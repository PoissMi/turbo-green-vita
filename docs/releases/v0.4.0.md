## Highlights

On-device profiling identified the render path — not the decoder — as the
bottleneck: the CPU copy that uploads each 720p NV12 frame out of the
uncached CDRAM staging buffers ran at ~69 MB/s (20.5 ms/frame) and pinned
the display near 30 fps while the decoder sustained 60. This release moves
that upload onto the Vita DMA engine (**20,481 µs → 1,057 µs** on device)
and takes simple scenes from 60/30 to 60/58 presented/decoded. Complex,
high-motion scenes are now bounded by network/encoder jitter rather than
anything the client renders.

## Streaming & performance

- **DMA-based NV12 upload.** `SDL_UpdateNVTexture`'s CPU memcpy is replaced
  by `sceDmacMemcpy` on the DMA engine (Y and UV planes into the locked
  streaming texture). Bypasses the CPU caches entirely.
- **Per-texture GXM scene tracking.** Three rotating display textures
  replace the single streaming texture; `sceGxmFinish` now only waits for
  the scene that sampled the texture being reused, so GPU work overlaps CPU.
- **Overlay throttling with cached replay.** While streaming, egui rebuilds
  at 10 Hz; video still presents every iteration. The debug HUD replays its
  last geometry instead of strobing, in bright green monospace on a solid
  dark chip (previously white-on-transparent and unreadable over video).
- **Decode priority + 15 ms loop deadline.** The decode thread is raised to
  `SDL_THREAD_PRIORITY_HIGH` on its pinned core, and the loop deadline is
  tightened so late-decode frames land closer to the vsync edge.
- **Decode-result queue + drain-after-receive.** Results move through a
  depth-3 queue (no more single-slot overwrites), and the RTC pump drains
  *after* receiving RTP so freshly decoded frames are picked up in the same
  cycle. `repl` 11 → 2, pipeline age halved in complex scenes.
- **TransportLayerNack loss recovery.** NACK feedback was negotiated but
  never sent; it is now sent for missing sequence numbers (16-bit bitmap
  pairs). New `nack:` counter in the HUD alongside `drop:` and `err:`.
- **Two decoder reference frames**(down from four) for faster hardware
  decode; no motion corruption was observed on device.
- **HUD additions:** `pr` (blocking present time), `loop` (loop-start to
  present-return), `slot` (staging-slot misses) — the counters that found
  the upload bottleneck.

## UX

- **No CHOOSE MODE screen.** After sign-in you go straight into the Cloud
  title list. The Home (console) path is left compiled but unreachable;
  back-navigation now returns to the title list.
- Settings gained a **Max bitrate** selector (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 Mbps,
  default 2) advertised to xCloud via the client-capabilities message and
  SDP.

## Install

1. Download `turbo-green-vita.vpk`.
2. Transfer it to the Vita and install it with VitaShell.
3. Enable **Unsafe Homebrew** in HENkaku Settings so Turbo GreenVita can
   access the hardware video-decoder module.
4. Launch Turbo GreenVita and complete the Xbox device-code sign-in.

## Test focus

Verify stream startup, colors, aspect ratio and crop, pause/resume,
disconnect/reconnect, and repeated stream starts. In Settings → Stream debug
info, confirm `mem:PHYCONT-NC`, `out:NV12`, and that complex scenes keep
`drop` low with `nack` climbing under loss.
